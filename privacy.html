Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Fortune Studio LLC (“we”, “us”), a photography studio and event-rental business in Scranton, Pennsylvania. This policy explains what we collect when you visit the site, book a service or contact us.

2. Information we collect

  • Booking details: your name, phone number, email address, the service, date and time you choose, and any delivery address or notes you give us.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We receive confirmation that a payment was made and the amount.
  • Messages: your name, email address and the messages you send through the website chat/contact form, by email or by phone, plus our replies to you.
  • Visit information: your approximate country and city (taken from your internet connection), device type, the website you came from, how long you stay and how far you scroll. Our own visit statistics do not store your IP address.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, which may use cookies and collect information such as your IP address and browser, under Google’s own privacy policy.

3. How we use it

To take and confirm bookings, process payments, send confirmation emails, deliver rentals and photography services, answer your questions, manage website-chat conversations and send replies, understand how the website is used, improve it, and prevent fraud or abuse.

4. Customer support messages

Our website chat connects you directly with a real Fortune Studio team member. When you contact us, your name, email address and messages are stored in our private customer-support inbox using Supabase. We use Resend to notify our team and send replies to your email address. A secure browser token lets the same browser reopen your conversation and display our replies in the website chat. Our team may not be online immediately, but you can leave a message and we will reply when available. Please do not share card numbers or other sensitive information in the chat.

5. Cookies and similar technology

We use a random visit identifier stored in your browser session to count visits, and we remember your language and cookie choice. Stripe and Google may set their own cookies. When you choose “Decline” in the cookie notice, we stop our analytics on that browser and ask Google Analytics not to collect data.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business: Stripe (payments), Resend (emails), Supabase (website data and customer support messages), Vercel (hosting), and Google (analytics and maps). We may also share information when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records as long as needed to provide the service and meet business, tax and legal requirements. Visit statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for understanding how the site is used.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below. You can also decline analytics cookies in the cookie notice or in your browser settings.

9. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS) and trusted providers. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
