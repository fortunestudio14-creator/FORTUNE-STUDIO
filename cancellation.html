Cancellation & Refunds

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Changing or canceling a booking

Please contact us as soon as possible by phone or email. Tell us your name, the service and your booking date so we can find it quickly. The earlier you tell us, the easier it is for us to help.

Rescheduling

We will do our best to move your booking to another date. A new date depends on availability.

Refunds

We review every refund request fairly. When a refund is approved, it is returned to the original payment method. Your bank may need several business days to show it.

Deposits

A deposit holds your date, and we may turn away other customers for it, so a deposit may not be refundable. We will review your situation and explain our decision.

If we need to cancel

If we must cancel because of illness, severe weather or another situation we cannot avoid, we will offer you a new date or a full refund of what you paid for that booking.

Payments that were not completed

A date is reserved only after your payment is confirmed. If a payment fails or is canceled during checkout, your date is not reserved and you are not charged.
